# 🎯 ML Interview Question Bank

## Fundamentals
1. Bias vs. variance — how do you diagnose and fix each?
2. L1 vs. L2 regularisation — effect on weights?
3. Why do we split into train/validation/test? What is data leakage?
4. Precision vs. recall — when do you prefer each?
5. Explain cross-validation and when stratified/time-series splits are needed.
6. How do you handle imbalanced classes?

## Algorithms
7. How does a decision tree choose a split (Gini vs. entropy)?
8. Why does Random Forest reduce variance? What is bagging?
9. Boosting vs. bagging?
10. What is the kernel trick in SVM?
11. Why is Naive Bayes "naive" and why does it still work?
12. How do you choose k in KNN / K-Means (elbow, silhouette)?
13. DBSCAN: what do `eps` and `min_samples` control?
14. Why does logistic regression use log-loss rather than MSE?

## Deep learning
15. Explain backpropagation.
16. Vanishing/exploding gradients and mitigations.
17. Batch norm vs. layer norm; dropout at train vs. inference.
18. Self-attention: what are queries, keys, values?

## Practical
19. Your model is great offline but poor in production — why?
20. How would you monitor a deployed model for drift?
